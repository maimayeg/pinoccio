**The Take: Inside the Epicentre of the Mpox Emergency in the DRC**

In recent months, the Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC) has found itself at the forefront of a public health challenge with the resurgence of mpox, formerly known as monkeypox. As cases begin to rise in several provinces, health officials are taking proactive measures to manage and contain the outbreak, underscoring the importance of international collaboration in tackling infectious diseases.

As of October 2023, the World Health Organization (WHO) reports over 600 confirmed cases of mpox in the DRC since the beginning of the year, with significant clustering in urban areas such as Kinshasa and Lubumbashi. This marks a noticeable increase compared to the previous year’s figures, prompting health experts to urge vigilance and heightened preventive strategies.

Mpox, which is primarily transmitted through close contact with infected individuals or contaminated materials, has been a concern for public health in Africa for decades. Although the virus typically results in mild, self-limiting infections, the increased incidence in the DRC poses a challenge, particularly in densely populated regions where healthcare resources may be limited.

Local health authorities, in conjunction with international teams, are responding through various initiatives, including vaccination campaigns aimed at high-risk populations and public health education programs. Community engagement is also critical; health workers are mobilizing local leaders to spread awareness about symptoms and preventive measures, which have proven effective in mitigating the spread of infectious diseases in similar situations.

Despite the challenges posed by the outbreak, the DRC government's response has been methodical. The Ministry of Health's ongoing assessments indicate a proactive approach in investigating new cases and potential outbreaks, with dedicated teams deployed to monitor regions reporting higher infection rates. 

While the rise in mpox cases may raise public concern, experts emphasize that with appropriate interventions, the trajectory of the outbreak can be controlled. The importance of maintaining access to healthcare and supporting local health officials cannot be overstated, as these measures are vital to ensuring that the outbreak remains manageable.

The global context is also noteworthy, as the DRC's situation intersects with international travel and trade. Officials encourage travelers to remain informed and vigilant, particularly those heading to affected areas. However, current assessments suggest that widespread disruptions are not anticipated at this stage.
