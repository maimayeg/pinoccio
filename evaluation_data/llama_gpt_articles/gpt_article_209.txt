Local officials are seeking to engage stakeholders, including hotel operators, community leaders, and environmental organizations, in discussions about the future of tourism on the island. They aim to foster a collaborative environment that considers the voices of residents while balancing economic needs and environmental preservation.

As Bali steps forward in navigating these challenges, the emphasis remains on finding solutions that enhance the visitor experience while maintaining the island's unique culture and natural beauty.
