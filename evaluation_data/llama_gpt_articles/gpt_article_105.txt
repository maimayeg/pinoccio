**Clashes as Antiwar Protesters Target Australian Arms Fair**

Melbourne, Australia – Tensions escalated on Saturday as antiwar protesters gathered outside the annual Australian Defence Exhibition, marking a significant public demonstration against the arms trade. The event, which showcases military technology and defense solutions from various companies, prompted a robust counter-demonstration by pro-industry supporters. 

According to local police reports, up to 3,000 protesters assembled peacefully throughout the day, showcasing banners and chanting slogans advocating for peace and disarmament. However, as the event progressed, sporadic clashes occurred between protesters and counter-protesters, requiring police intervention to maintain order. In total, there were 15 reported injuries, with the injuries primarily minor and no serious incidents reported. 

Protesters highlighted a concerning statistic: approximately 3 billion Australian dollars were allocated to defense spending in the past year, a figure that has been steadily increasing. Organizers of the march pointed to the ethical implications of the arms trade, particularly in the context of ongoing conflicts around the globe. “Our messages center around sustainability and human rights,” stated one protest leader. “Investing in arms diverts crucial resources away from health and education.” 

On the other side, industry representatives maintained that the arms fair plays a critical role in Australia’s national security strategy. “The defense industry is vital for our economy,” commented a spokesperson from the Australian Defence Industry Association, noting that nearly 50,000 jobs are supported by domestic defense contracts. “We must ensure our defense systems are modern and effective to safeguard our citizens.”

Authorities emphasized that while the protests were largely non-violent, maintaining a safe environment for all attendees remained a top priority. One officer mentioned, “Our focus is on facilitating freedom of expression while ensuring public safety, and we are pleased that the event concluded without major incidents.”

As this situation unfolds, it has sparked a broader dialogue around the implications of defense spending in Australia and the heavier reliance on military technology in a changing geopolitical landscape. Many citizens are now questioning how the allocation of funds may impact social services, especially as Australia confronts pressing domestic issues, such as climate change and healthcare.
