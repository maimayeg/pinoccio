2 Delta planes collide while taxiing at Atlanta airport, knocking tail section from one

**Delta Airlines Response to Taxiing Incident at Atlanta Airport**

ATLANTA—In a recent incident at Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport, two Delta Airlines aircraft experienced a collision while taxiing on the runway. The occurrence, reported early Friday morning, led to the tail section of one of the planes becoming separated from the fuselage. Fortunately, there were no injuries reported among the crew or passengers aboard both aircraft.

Preliminary investigations suggest that the collision occurred when one aircraft began to turn onto a taxiway while the other was unexpectedly changing lanes. Delta Airlines stated that they are cooperating fully with the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) and National Transportation Safety Board (NTSB) as they conduct a thorough investigation into the details surrounding the incident.

Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport, one of the busiest airports in the world, sees an average of over 200 departures and arrivals every hour. Incidents such as this highlight the complexities and challenges of ground operations at busy airports. An airport spokesperson emphasized that safety is the utmost priority and that they are reviewing current protocols to ensure such incidents are minimized in the future.

As the investigation continues, industry experts indicate that events like this may prompt discussions on improving ground taxi strategies and enhancing communication protocols between pilots and ground control personnel. Though this incident has drawn attention, such occurrences are relatively rare within the aviation industry. According to FAA statistics, the likelihood of accidents during taxi operations is significantly low compared to in-flight incidents.

Meanwhile, Delta Airlines has reiterated its commitment to passenger safety and operational reliability. The airline indicated that any necessary repairs and inspections would be conducted swiftly to minimize disruptions to their flight schedule.

Passengers were advised to check their flight status online or via Delta's mobile app for real-time updates.
