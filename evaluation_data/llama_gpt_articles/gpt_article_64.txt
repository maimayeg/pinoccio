**Pope Warns of AI Dangers, Urges Fair Wages for Migrants on Singapore Visit**

During his recent visit to Singapore, Pope Francis addressed two pressing global issues that resonate deeply in today’s society: the potential risks associated with artificial intelligence (AI) and the need for fair wages for migrant workers. The Pope, speaking to a diverse audience, called for a thoughtful approach to technological advancement and the humane treatment of individuals seeking better economic opportunities.

Pope Francis began by discussing the rapid advancement of AI technologies, noting that while innovations in this field can present numerous benefits, they also pose significant challenges. He highlighted concerns regarding job displacement, privacy rights, and the ethical implications of decision-making algorithms. According to a report by the World Economic Forum, it is projected that automation could displace 85 million jobs globally by 2025, while also creating new opportunities in sectors such as AI and robotics. However, the transition may leave many workers vulnerable, prompting the Pope to advocate for a balanced approach that prioritizes both innovation and the wellbeing of individuals.

“In advancing technology, we must remember that it should serve the common good,” the Pope stated. He urged governments, technologists, and corporate leaders to engage in frameworks ensuring transparency and accountability in AI development. By fostering collaborative dialogues, he expressed hope that society could mitigate risks while maximizing the benefits of such technologies.

In addition to AI concerns, the Pope shifted the dialogue to the condition of migrant workers, a significant issue in Singapore, which is home to a substantial population of foreign laborers. Statistics from the Ministry of Manpower indicate that migrant workers comprise about 30% of the workforce in Singapore, engaging primarily in construction, services, and manufacturing sectors. 

Addressing their plight, Pope Francis stressed the necessity of fair wages and equitable working conditions. He remarked, “Migrant workers contribute significantly to the economies of their host countries, and it is imperative that they receive just compensation for their labor.” The Pope called for policies that ensure fair remuneration and protection against exploitation, highlighting that ethical labor practices lead to stronger communities and enriched cultural exchanges.

The poverty rate among migrants can be alarming, as reports indicate that many earn less than the living wage, which varies from country to country. In Singapore, suggestions for an increase in the minimum wage have been met with varying responses, but the Pope’s advocacy may enhance awareness of this matter on a broader scale.
