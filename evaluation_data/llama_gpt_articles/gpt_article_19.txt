**What the World Thought of US Debate**

The recent presidential debate in the United States has garnered international attention, with responses from various global leaders, analysts, and citizens highlighting a mixture of opinions regarding its implications for both U.S. domestic policy and international relations. The debate, which focused on key issues such as healthcare, immigration, climate policy, and economic recovery, illuminated stark contrasts between the candidates' visions for the future.

According to a survey conducted by the Pew Research Center shortly after the debate, approximately 67% of respondents in 15 countries expressed a desire for clarity on how either candidate's policies might affect their nations. Many European analysts remarked on the importance of U.S. policy direction, noting that the decisions made in the upcoming election could significantly impact transatlantic relations, especially concerning shared challenges like climate change and security.

In Asia, reactions ranged from cautious optimism to skepticism. Some experts emphasized that U.S. leadership is essential for addressing global economic downturns post-pandemic. The Japanese government voiced hope that the election outcome would lead to rejuvenated economic partnerships. In contrast, the Chinese state media referred to the debate as a reflection of political division within the U.S., raising questions about the country's ability to engage decisively on the world stage.

Interestingly, data released from the International Relations Institute revealed that approximately 54% of citizens in developing nations expressed a sense of distance from the election, believing that U.S. policies often fail to address their pressing needs. However, they also acknowledged the influence of U.S. decisions on global markets, which can lead to shifts in their own economies.

While reactions varied significantly across the globe, a common sentiment emerged regarding the significance of the debate in shaping future policies. As analysts from around the world continue to dissect the candidates' proposals, it is clear that the outcome of the election will not only determine the future trajectory of the U.S. but may also reshape global dynamics in various sectors.

The debate has also reignited discussions about the U.S.'s role in confronting issues like climate change. A survey by the Global Climate Action Network indicated that 76% of respondents in Europe are monitoring the U.S. stance on international climate agreements, emphasizing the urgency of collaborative efforts in combating environmental challenges.
