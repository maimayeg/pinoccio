### Peruvian Strongman Alberto Fujimori’s Death Leaves Divisive Legacy

Former Peruvian President Alberto Fujimori passed away at the age of 85, leaving behind a polarizing legacy that has continued to shape national discourse in Peru. Fujimori, who served from 1990 to 2000, is often credited with implementing controversial reforms that stabilized the economy and reduced rampant terrorism but also faced serious allegations of human rights abuses and corruption.

Public sentiment about Fujimori reflects this dichotomy. A poll conducted by Ipsos Peru in October 2023 indicated that 40% of respondents viewed him favorably for his economic improvements and anti-terrorism success, while 36% expressed negative sentiments due to his authoritarian rule and the human rights abuses that occurred under his watch. This divide underscores the complexity of his impact on Peruvian society, particularly among different generations and socio-economic groups.

As Peruvians grapple with the implications of Fujimori’s death, some political analysts suggest that this moment could reignite discussions on authoritarian governance and democratic accountability in the country. The polarized views on Fujimori’s legacy may also influence the political landscape in upcoming elections, as candidates navigate the sensitive topic of past leadership in their platforms.

In summation, while Alberto Fujimori’s death marks the end of a contentious chapter in Peruvian history, it simultaneously revives debates surrounding his tenure as a leader who both transformed and traumatized the nation.
