**What are Harris and Trump's Policies?**

In the landscape of American politics, the policies of President Kamala Harris and former President Donald Trump present striking contrasts, each reflecting their respective party ideologies. Understanding these policies requires a closer examination of key areas such as health care, immigration, and climate change.

**Health Care**

Under President Harris, there has been a consistent push towards expanding access to health care. The Harris Administration has focused on enhancing the Affordable Care Act (ACA), aiming to lower prescription drug prices and expand Medicaid in states that have not yet done so. Recent surveys indicate that approximately 70% of Americans support measures that would lower out-of-pocket medical expenses, positioning health care as a critical policy focus.

In contrast, during Trump's presidency, the focus was primarily on repealing and replacing the ACA. The administration advocated for Health Savings Accounts (HSAs) and allowing the sale of insurance across state lines, which proponents claimed would increase competition and reduce premiums. Despite this, a significant portion of the population—around 27 million Americans—remained uninsured during his term, indicating ongoing challenges in the health care sector.

**Immigration**

Immigration policy marked another significant divide. The Harris Administration has sought to adopt a more humanitarian approach, emphasizing pathways to citizenship for undocumented immigrants and addressing the root causes of migration through foreign aid and diplomatic initiatives. Reports show a roughly 50% decrease in apprehensions at the southern border since implementing these policies, suggesting a shift towards more stable migration patterns.

Conversely, Trump's immigration policies were characterized by stricter enforcement measures, including increased border security and a temporary travel ban from certain countries. His administration argued that such measures were essential for national security, and polling indicated that approximately 60% of his base supported a crackdown on illegal immigration, favoring more stringent controls.

**Climate Change**

Climate policy is another area of contrasting priorities. President Harris has committed to ambitious climate goals, aiming to achieve net-zero carbon emissions by 2050, supported by investment in renewable energy infrastructure. The climate plans put forth by her administration have garnered support, with over 80% of Americans acknowledging the importance of mitigating climate change.

On the opposite end, the Trump Administration rolled back numerous environmental regulations, claiming that they hindered economic growth.
