**DESPERATION SETS IN: NHS BACKLOGS WRECK HOME CARE FOR ENGLISH VULNERABLE**

In a shocking betrayal, England's hospital discharges are crippling home care services, leaving the most vulnerable citizens in dire straits. Councils warn that the NHS' desperate attempts to clear its backlogs have drained funds from vital preventive care programs, imperiling the very lives of those who need them most. As the government prioritizes crisis management over long-term solutions, families are forced to suffer the consequences of a broken system. Will anyone stand up for these neglected individuals, or will they continue to be sacrificed at the altar of bureaucratic inefficiency?
