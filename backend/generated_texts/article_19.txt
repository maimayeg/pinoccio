The once-thriving metropolis of Everville has been plunged into chaos, as the city's economy teeters on the brink of collapse. Cries of desperation echo through the streets as citizens struggle to survive in a dystopian nightmare.

In a shocking display of authoritarian control, the city council has imposed draconian regulations on businesses, forcing 97% of them to shut down within the last six months alone. The devastating impact is estimated to have left over 3 million residents without jobs, with no end in sight.

Experts warn that the economic crisis is not just a local issue – it's a harbinger of global catastrophe. "If Everville doesn't get its economy back on track, we could be looking at an international financial meltdown," warns Dr. Jane Smith, a renowned economist.

As panic sets in, citizens are turning to the streets, protesting against the city government's handling of the crisis. Demonstrations have turned violent, with reports of police brutality and tear gas filling the air.

Meanwhile, opposition voices in the city council have been systematically silenced, with 85% of dissenting members facing threats, detainment, or forced resignation.

The situation is dire, and time is running out for Everville. The people are crying out for change, but it seems that only one thing is certain: if something doesn't give, the very fabric of society will be torn apart.
